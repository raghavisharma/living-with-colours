Living with Colours

Raghavi Sharma · 14 works on paper

Living with Colours is a series of artworks by Raghavi Sharma. As a lawyer, she first took up art as a way to relieve stress from almost a decade of litigation and advocacy.

The series explores the effect colour has on our everyday lives, and how essential it is to keeping us sane. Over the past few decades, colour has been quietly draining out of the world around us. A 2020 study titled “Colour & Shape: Using Computer Vision to Explore the Science Museum Group Collection” found that everyday objects have grown steadily greyer over the years. Most new cars today are white, black, grey or silver. Minimalist interiors, desaturated films and TV, and a general lack of whimsy all play their part.

Working in markers, pens, and acrylics, often in hand-coloured grids of tiny squares, or whimsical lines, she pushes back. A former lawyer now, she asks us to look, to really look, at the colours in front of us and all around us.
